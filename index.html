Step 1: Original Question (2014 DSE Math I Q16)

In Figure 5, the 1st pattern consists of 3 dots. For any positive integer \( n \), the \( (n + 1) \)-th pattern is formed by adding 2 dots to the \( n \)-th pattern. Find the least value of \( m \) such that the total number of dots in the first \( m \) patterns exceeds 6888.

Solution:

Let \( T(n) \) be the number of dots in the \( n \)-th pattern.

The sequence of dots is an arithmetic sequence with the first term \( a = 3 \) and common difference \( d = 2 \).

The total number of dots in the first \( m \) patterns is the sum of the first \( m \) terms, \( S(m) \):

\[ S(m) = \frac{m}{2} [2a + (m-1)d] \] \[ S(m) = \frac{m}{2} [2(3) + (m-1)2] \] \[ S(m) = \frac{m}{2} [6 + 2m - 2] = m(m + 2) = m^2 + 2m \]

We are given that \( S(m) > 6888 \):

\[ m^2 + 2m > 6888 \] \[ m^2 + 2m - 6888 > 0 \] \[ (m - 82)(m + 84) > 0 \]

Since \( m \) must be a positive integer (\( m > 0 \)), we have \( m > 82 \).

Therefore, the least value of \( m \) is 83.
